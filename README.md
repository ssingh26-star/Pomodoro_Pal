# 🦊 Pomodoropal

A cute, gamified Pomodoro productivity app that helps you focus, track tasks, build study streaks, and stay motivated with a virtual pet companion.

Built entirely with **vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript** — no frameworks, no build step, no backend.

## ✨ Features

### ⏱️ Pomodoro Timer
- Classic 25-minute focus sessions with 5-minute short breaks and 15-minute long breaks
- Start, pause, and reset controls
- Auto-starts a break after each completed focus session
- Focus sessions are logged to your stats

### 📝 To-Do List
- Add, complete, and delete tasks
- Filter by **All**, **Active**, or **Completed**
- Clear all completed tasks at once
- Completing a task boosts your pet's happiness and counts toward achievements

### 📅 Study Check-in Calendar
- Monthly calendar view with check-in tracking
- Automatic **streak calculation** for consecutive days
- Monthly progress bar

### 📊 Cute Stats
- Day streak, total check-ins, focus sessions, and tasks completed
- Progress bars toward long-term goals
- Weekly activity grid
- **Mood tracker** — log how you're feeling each day

### 🐾 Virtual Pet Companion
- Choose from a fox, cat, dog, panda, or koala
- Pet has **happiness** and **energy** stats that respond to your activity
- Click to interact and get encouraging messages
- Pet reacts to your mood and productivity

### 🏆 Achievements
- 9 unlockable badges, including:
  - **First Step** — complete your first check-in
  - **Week Warrior** — 7-day streak
  - **Monthly Master** — 30-day streak
  - **Focus Expert** — 50 focus sessions
  - **Productivity Legend** — 100 tasks completed

### 🏅 Leaderboard
- Compete with other users on **streak**, **check-ins**, and **focus sessions**
- Top 10 rankings plus your personal rank card

### ⚙️ Settings
- Dark / Light mode toggle
- Font size options
- Sound effects and browser notifications
- Pet type selection

### 🔐 User Accounts
- Register and log in (stored locally)
- Per-user data: todos, stats, check-ins, mood history, achievements, and settings
- **Guest mode** — browse and use todos without an account
- Includes a demo account: username `demo`, password `demo123`
